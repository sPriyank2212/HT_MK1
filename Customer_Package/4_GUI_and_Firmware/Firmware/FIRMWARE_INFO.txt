Changes in this build (see 0_Documents/6_Code_Walkthrough, Known Limitations)
  - the HV rail is checked before and after every insulation reading, and proven discharged
    before the relays open
  - any HV-path error latches a fault (F01 relay bus, F02 DAC/ADC, F03 rail) and stops the run
  - an idle HV rail latches a fault; stack overflow and allocation failure reset through the watchdog
  - HV SET no longer reports a level it did not set; resistance and continuity report bus errors

Flashing
  Follow 0_Documents/4_Flashing_Procedure.pdf, then run its PING / PONG check before testing.
  Afterwards the GUI should connect and show "fw 0.1.0" in the status bar.

Not yet verified on hardware
  This image has been compiled and its pure logic unit-tested on a PC, but not exercised on a
  unit. The resistance and insulation paths, the HV rail thresholds and the stack sizes are still
  to be confirmed on real hardware (see the Functional Test Procedure and PROJECT_LOG.md BU-13 to BU-15).
  There is also no hardware interlock signal.
