1. Control Card (IHT-MKI-CC)
The Control Card makes +5 V, +3.3 V and two isolated +5 V rails. It is powered through J7 only, not from USB or JTAG, with no power jumper soldered. Prove the rails before connecting any other card.
Reading rules for every resistance table: power off and cards unmated; red lead on the Red (+) pin, black lead on the Black (−) pin; for rail-to-ground rows swap the leads and record the lower reading. Let decoupling capacitors charge before reading. A steady reading below about 10 Ω between different nets is a short; open means above about 1 MΩ. These limits are guide values, not from the schematics.
