1. System Overview
The Harness Tester performs electrical verification of wire harness assemblies by applying controlled voltages and measuring responses across 256 lines (High Side) to 256 lines (Low Side). The system comprises three cards that must be initialized in sequence before any test operation commences.

1.3 HV Insulation Voltage Divider — Principle of Operation
The HV Card sense network uses a 4-resistor chain to monitor the applied HV and to detect insulation faults. The key measurement resistors are:
• R_series = 1 MΩ  (R3001 + R3002 combined — series path from HV line)
• R_bottom = 1 kΩ  (R3004 — fixed ADC-side resistor)
Worst-case condition — direct wire connection (zero insulation impedance):
When the harness wire under test has zero impedance to the return (i.e., a dead short or direct connection), the 1 MΩ series resistor and 1 kΩ bottom resistor form a simple divider:
At the 10 MΩ insulation go/no-go limit the leakage develops ≈ 0.045 V across R3004 (1 kΩ) at the HV_RET node. Good insulation (> 10 MΩ) reads below 0.045 V; a low-impedance/short reads well above. (HV_Sense, the separate rail-monitor node, reads ≈ 0.245 V at 500 V — see §3.2.)
At a dead short (zero insulation impedance) the reading is 500 V × 1 kΩ / (1 MΩ + 1 kΩ) ≈ 0.50 V — this, not 0.049 V, is the true worst-case maximum (the earlier 0.049 V figure was a ×10 error; only the 0.045 V = 10 MΩ go/no-go point was correct). Good wire insulation means the insulation impedance is very high (≥ 10 MΩ), so virtually no current flows and the voltage across the 1 kΩ bottom resistor drops to near zero.

FIRMWARE REVIEW NOTE (v1.3): Bands re-derived from the actual divider V_R3004 = 500 V × 1 kΩ / (1 MΩ + R_ins + 1 kΩ). Estimate insulation resistance from the reading as R_ins ≈ 500000 / V_ADC − 1.001 MΩ. The single go/no-go stays at 0.045 V (=10 MΩ): below = PASS, at/above = FAIL. The full 0–0.50 V span (not 0–0.049 V) is available on the HV-card AD7476 (Vref = 5 V).
⚠  NOTE: Key principle: In this insulation test circuit a LOWER ADC voltage means BETTER insulation. GO/NO-GO: V_HV_RET < 0.045 V => PASS (>10 MΩ); >= 0.045 V => FAIL (<=10 MΩ). The graded bands are informational only. The ADC reading rises toward ~0.50 V as insulation resistance falls. A reading approaching ~0.50 V indicates a direct connection or insulation breakdown.

2. Control Card Initialization
The Control Card (STM32G474RETx) is the master of the entire system. All communication buses must be verified before proceeding to HV Card or Matrix Card operations.
2.1 Power-On Self Test (POST)
Step 2.1.1: Apply +5 V supply to Control Card. Confirm 3.3 V LDO (TLV70033) output is stable.
Step 2.1.2: Confirm isolated 5 V supplies (R05C05TE05S-R, R05CT05S-R) are active.
Step 2.1.3: Confirm +12 V boost (LT8337EV-1-PBF) output is stable.
Step 2.1.4: uC firmware boot executes. Initialize all GPIO to safe defaults:
◦ All MCP23017 I2C write pending — defer until bus init (Section 2.2)
◦ All SPI chip-select lines → HIGH (deselected)
◦ DAC8775 RESET_N → deassert (HIGH = active)
◦ OPTO_CNTR (PC12) → LOW (Opto SPDT selects 3.3 V pull-up path, not DAC path)
2.2 I2C Bus Initialization
Step 2.2.1: Configure I2C2 and I2C3 peripherals at 100 kHz (standard mode). Isolators (ADUM1205) are active on both buses.
Step 2.2.2: Perform I2C bus scan (0x00–0x7F) on I2C2 (HV Card bus) and I2C3 (Matrix Card bus).
Step 2.2.3: Verify DAC8830 responds on its isolated SPI (write-only device — no address).
Step 2.2.4: Verify all 8× MCP23017 on HV Card respond on I2C2 at addresses 0x20–0x27. Log each.
Step 2.2.5: Verify 2× MCP23017 on Matrix Card respond on I2C3. Log addresses.
Step 2.2.6: If any expected device does not respond: log "I2C device missing at address 0xXX on bus Y". HALT.
⚠  NOTE: I2C buses use ADUM1205 bidirectional isolators. Pull-up resistors (4.7 kΩ) are on both sides of the isolator. If scan returns no devices, verify isolator power supply (+3.3 V side 1, +5 V side 2) before assuming firmware fault.
2.3 SPI Bus Initialization — ADC Channels
Step 2.3.1: Configure SPI2 (isolated HV-card ADCs U301/U302), SPI1 (Matrix ADC U33, HI_COM) and SPI3 (Control-Card ADC U4): Mode 0, appropriate clock per AD7476 datasheet.
Step 2.3.2: Assert CS for each ADC, perform a dummy read cycle, deassert CS. Verify 12-bit response word is not all-zeros or all-ones.
Step 2.3.3: Perform baseline ADC read on all channels with no input stimulus:
◦ HV feedback channel (isolated SPI2): expected near 0 V (HV off, no current in divider)
◦ Continuity channels (SPI3 via Opto SPDT in pull-up mode): expected 3.3 V
Step 2.3.4: Log baseline values. Any anomalous baseline indicates a hardware fault.

3. HV Card Initialization
⚠  NOTE: HIGH VOLTAGE HAZARD — up to 500 VDC. Ensure test fixture is fully enclosed and no personnel contact is possible with HV lines before and during this section.
3.1 Safety State — GPIO Expander All-Zero Before HV Enable
Before the CA05P-5 DCDC is enabled, all 8× MCP23017 GPIO expanders on the HV Card must be written to output 0x00 on every port. This guarantees all 128 reed relay driver transistors (64 High Side + 64 Low Side) are OFF. There is no relay status feedback line — the firmware enforces safety by commanding all outputs low and trusting the open-drain / pull-down circuit design.
Step 3.1.1: For each MCP23017 at addresses 0x20 through 0x27 via I2C2:
◦ Write IODIR register A = 0xFF (configure as inputs first to avoid glitch, then switch)
◦ Write OLAT register A = 0x00
◦ Write OLAT register B = 0x00
◦ Write IODIR register A = 0x00 (all port A pins → output)
◦ Write IODIR register B = 0x00 (all port B pins → output)
◦ Readback OLAT A and OLAT B: confirm both = 0x00
Step 3.1.2: If any readback ≠ 0x00: log "MCP23017 write fail at address 0xXX". HALT — do not enable HV.
Step 3.1.3: Read ADC on HV feedback channel (isolated SPI2). Confirm reading < 0.005 V (HV off, residual only).
⚠  NOTE: The 2N7002 gate is held LOW by the 10 kΩ pull-down resistor when MCP23017 output = 0. With gate LOW, the 2N7002 is OFF and no current flows through the reed relay coil. This is the inherently safe state.
3.2 DAC8830 Configuration and HV Ramp
Step 3.2.1: Via the isolated SPI, write DAC8830 = 0x0000 (minimum output → 0 V reference to CA05P-5 VPGM pin).
Step 3.2.2: (DAC8830 is write-only; there is no register readback — command 0x0000 and proceed.)
Step 3.2.3: Enable CA05P-5 (if EN pin is firmware-controlled). DCDC begins ramping from 0 V.
Step 3.2.4: Increment DAC in controlled steps — 10% of full-scale per step, 100 ms hold per step — to allow DCDC and RC network to stabilize.
Step 3.2.5: At each step, read the HV_Sense rail-monitor ADC (isolated SPI2). The divider gives V_HV_Sense ≈ 0.00049 × V_HV (≈ 0.245 V at 500 V):

4. HV Card — Harness Insulation Test
With 500 V established, the insulation integrity of the harness is tested wire by wire. Each High Side line is energized through the HV reed relay (via the 1 MΩ series resistor chain) while the low-side return path is closed (see Sec. 4.0 REVIEW NOTE) and all other high-side reed relays remain OFF. The ADC monitors the voltage across the 1 kΩ bottom resistor. A near-zero reading confirms good insulation. A rising voltage toward ~0.50 V indicates current leakage and thus insulation degradation or failure.
FIRMWARE REVIEW NOTE (v1.3): Measurement loop / return path. The reading is the voltage across R3004 (1 kΩ) at HV_RET; current flows only when the loop is closed: +500 V → R3002 (1 MΩ) → HS reed relay[n] → HS wire → [insulation] → an adjacent conductor → its LS reed relay → HV_RET → R3004 (1 kΩ) → GND. RETURN SCHEME: energise the HS relay of the net under test and CLOSE ALL LOW-SIDE relays EXCEPT the LS relay(s) belonging to that same net (its own return, per the connection map). Every other conductor is thereby tied to HV_RET, so the energised net is measured against all of them, while excluding its own return prevents a good connection from reading as leakage. With NO low-side relay closed there is no loop and every line reads ~0 V (false PASS).
FIRMWARE REVIEW NOTE (v1.3): Line count. One HV card tests 64 HS lines (not 256). Run the per-line loop for n = 1..64 on this card, then repeat for each additional HV card via its ISO_HV_CARD_ENx bank-select (see Sec. 1.1/1.2).
⚠  NOTE: No closed-loop feedback is used during the insulation test. The GPIO expanders are commanded to all-zero and individual reed relays are activated one at a time under direct firmware control. The ADC reading is the sole indicator of insulation condition.
4.1 Pre-Test Safety Verification
Step 4.1.1: Confirm all 8× MCP23017 OLAT registers = 0x00 (all reed relays OFF). Read back each expander.
Step 4.1.2: Confirm HV ADC channel reads ~0.245 V ± 0.010 V (500 V present, no load current).
Step 4.1.3: Confirm Matrix Card all outputs = 0x00 (no Matrix paths closed — Section 5 prerequisite).
Step 4.1.4: Prepare the low-side return. The insulation measurement needs every conductor EXCEPT the net under test tied to HV_RET. Accordingly, for each high-side line the procedure below closes all Low-Side reed relays except the LS relay(s) of that net (its own return, from the connection map). A closed return is mandatory — with all LS relays open there is no measurement loop and every line reads ~0 V (false PASS).
4.2 Sequential Insulation Test Procedure
Step 4.2.1: Load the harness under test. Identify all High Side lines on this HV card (up to 64; repeat the whole procedure per HV card for a full 256-line harness).
Step 4.2.2: For each High Side line HS[n], n = 1 to 64 (per HV card):
◦ Identify the MCP23017 address and port pin corresponding to HS_RELAY[n].
◦ Close all Low-Side reed relays EXCEPT the LS relay(s) of net n (its own return, per the connection map). This ties every other conductor to HV_RET as the return bundle.
◦ Write that single bit to 1 on the relevant MCP23017 OLAT register. All other outputs remain 0.
◦ Wait stabilization: ≥ 50 ms (allows RC settling with stray capacitance on the HV line).
◦ Read the HV_RET leakage ADC (isolated SPI2). This is V_ADC for HS[n].
◦ Evaluate result — see table below.
◦ Write that bit back to 0 (reed relay OFF). Wait 20 ms before next relay.

5. Matrix Card Initialization
The Matrix Card routes 256 High Side and 256 Low Side harness connections through 32× CD4067BF3A 16:1 analog multiplexers (16 MUX for HI, 16 MUX for LO), controlled by 2× MCP23017 GPIO expanders. The MUX enable (E pin — active LOW) and 4-bit address (S0–S3) lines select which of the 256 connections is active at any time.
5.1 GPIO Expander Discovery and All-Zero Init
Step 5.1.1: Via I2C3: scan for MCP23017 on Matrix Card. Expect 2 expanders:
◦ U101 — drives HI_EN1–16 (enables for the 16 High Side MUXes)
◦ U102 — drives LO_EN1–16 (enables for the 16 Low Side MUXes)
Step 5.1.2: For each expander: write all OLAT registers to 0x00, configure all IODIR = 0x00 (output). Readback to confirm.
Step 5.1.3: With all EN lines = 0 (active LOW), all 32 CD4067 MUXes are disabled — all 256 HI and 256 LO paths are open.
Step 5.1.4: Write shared MUX address lines: S0–S3 (HI and LO) = 0x00. These are driven from Control Card GPIO directly (not via expanders). Set to channel 0 as a defined default.
⚠  NOTE: CD4067 Enable (E) is active LOW. MCP23017 output = 0 means E = LOW = MUX enabled. Therefore all OLAT bits MUST be written HIGH (0xFF) to keep all MUXes DISABLED at init, not LOW. Confirm logic polarity in firmware before commanding expanders.
5.2 All-Paths-Open Verification
Step 5.2.1: With Control Card Opto SPDT in 3.3 V pull-up mode (OPTO_CNTR = LOW): all LS measurement nodes are pulled to 3.3 V.
Step 5.2.2: Read ADC (SPI3) on the continuity channel. With all Matrix paths open: expected ADC ≈ 3.3 V.
Step 5.2.3: Any reading significantly below 3.0 V indicates a stuck-enabled MUX, wiring fault, or load on the continuity line. Log and HALT.

6. Matrix Card — Harness Continuity Operations
The Control Card provides a 3.3 V pull-up on the ADC measurement input (via Opto SPDT in pull-up mode). When a Matrix path is closed — one CD4067 MUX enabled, one channel selected — the HS pin connects to the LS measurement node. If the harness wire is present, current flows and the ADC reads approximately 1.5 V. An open circuit (missing wire) reads 3.3 V.
FIRMWARE REVIEW NOTE (v1.3): LO_COM carries a 100 Ω pull-down to GND (hardware decision), which doubles as the current-source return. In continuity mode the "connected" node is the divider of the ~10 kΩ pull-up (Control, to 3.3 V) against (2·mux Rₒₙ + 100 Ω) — so a present wire reads WELL BELOW the old 1.5 V figure (a few hundred mV, or tens of mV with low-Rₒₙ muxes), while an open reads ≈ 3.3 V (pull-up only). Margin is large; set the connected/open thresholds from a bring-up loopback, not a fixed 1.5 V.

Selecting a path on the Matrix Card requires two steps:
• Step A — Write HI_EN or LO_EN bit HIGH (CD4067 E pin LOW = enabled) via MCP23017
• Step B — Set S0–S3 (4-bit channel address) on the enabled MUX to select one of 16 channels
The combination of MUX number (1–16) and channel address (0–15) gives 16 × 16 = 256 unique HS or LS connections.
6A. Operation A — Auto-Discovery Mode (256 × 256 Scan)
Purpose: Map all connections in an unknown 256-line harness. No pre-defined plan required. The system sequentially enables each of the 256 HS connections and reads all 256 LS paths.
Step 1: Confirm all Matrix paths disabled and ADC reads 3.3 V (from Section 5.2).
Step 2: Set OPTO_CNTR = LOW (3.3 V pull-up mode active).
Step 3: For each High Side connection HS[i], i = 1 to 256:
◦ Determine MUX number and channel: MUX_HI = ceil(i / 16), CH_HI = (i − 1) mod 16.
◦ Set HI_S0–S3 = CH_HI on Control Card GPIO.
◦ Enable MUX_HI: set HI_EN[MUX_HI] bit HIGH via U101 MCP23017.
◦ For each Low Side connection LS[j], j = 1 to 256:
     MUX_LO = ceil(j / 16),  CH_LO = (j − 1) mod 16
     Set LO_S0–S3 = CH_LO. Enable MUX_LO via U102.
     Read ADC. If reading ≈ 1.5 V → record (HS[i], LS[j]) as connected.
     Disable MUX_LO (LO_EN[MUX_LO] bit LOW via U102).
◦ Disable MUX_HI (HI_EN[MUX_HI] bit LOW via U101). Verify ADC returns to 3.3 V.
Step 4: Build connection map: list all (HS[i], LS[j]) pairs detected.
Step 5: Log: total connections, multi-destination HS pins, LS pins receiving from multiple HS sources.
Step 6: Output: Auto-discovered 256-line harness map. Save as reference plan for Operation B if required.
6B. Operation B — Pre-Defined Plan Verification Mode
Purpose: Verify a manufactured 256-line harness matches a known-good harness plan.
Step 1: Load harness plan from uC storage: list of expected (HS[i], LS[j]) pairs for all 256 connections.
Step 2: Confirm all Matrix paths disabled and ADC reads 3.3 V.
Step 3: For each expected connection (HS[i] → LS[j]) in the plan:
◦ Enable MUX_HI and set CH_HI; enable MUX_LO and set CH_LO.
◦ Read ADC: PASS if ≈ 1.5 V; FAIL-OPEN if ≈ 3.3 V (wire missing).
◦ Scan all other LS paths (or spot-check known non-connections): any ≈ 1.5 V reading is a FAULT-SHORT.
◦ Disable both MUXes before next connection.
Step 4: For each HS pin NOT in the plan: enable its MUX, scan all LO channels. Any 1.5 V reading = FAULT-EXTRA.
Step 5: Generate test report:
◦ PASS — connection detected, matches plan.
◦ OPEN FAIL — expected connection not detected (F06).
◦ SHORT FAIL — unplanned connection detected (F07).
7. Matrix Card — Harness Resistance Operations
Resistance measurement injects a known 10 mA current (from DAC8775 Channel A current-source mode on the Control Card) into each harness connection. The voltage drop across the wire is measured by the ADC. Ohm's Law gives: R = V_drop / 0.010 A.
FIRMWARE REVIEW NOTE (v1.3) — RESISTANCE PATH & CALIBRATION: Single-ended, 2-wire: U33 reads HI_COM to GND, so the reading = I × (2·mux Rₒₙ + 100 Ω LO_COM return + R_wire). The 100 Ω return lets current flow (the old 100 kΩ made 10 mA impossible). BUT compliance is separate from calibration: if mux Rₒₙ is high (classic CD4067B ≈ 500 Ω → ~1.1 kΩ offset) the node needs ~11 V at 10 mA and clamps at 3.3 V, so the current collapses no matter how you calibrate. Keep I × (offset + R_wire) < ~3.3 V via low-Rₒₙ muxes (~15 Ω) and/or lower current. THEN a reference-resistor self-calibration (planned: a permanent known-resistance path per side, selected by a GPIO-driven CD4067, measured before each test to characterise and subtract the offset) removes the offset and its drift, leaving the ADC step (LSB/I ≈ 80 mΩ at 10 mA) as the floor — good for connection/ohms-range resistance, not milliohm. IMPORTANT: the calibration path must traverse the SAME switches as the measurement path, else the main-mux Rₒₙ is not cancelled. For true milliohm precision, use 4-wire (Kelvin).

7.1 DAC8775 Current Source Setup
Step 7.1.1: Via SPI2, program DAC8775 Channel A to current-source mode. Set output to value corresponding to 10 mA.
Step 7.1.2: Set OPTO_CNTR (PC12) = HIGH. the Matrix ADC (SPI1, HI_COM) now reads the current-source output.
Step 7.1.3: Connect calibration resistor (100 Ω) across the measurement path. Expected ADC reading = 10 mA × 100 Ω = 1.000 V.
Step 7.1.4: If ADC reading deviates > 5% from 1.000 V: adjust DAC8775 value and repeat. Log correction factor (F10 if uncorrectable).
7A. Operation A — Auto-Resistance Discovery Mode
Purpose: Measure resistance of all 256 connections previously discovered in continuity Operation 6A.
Step 1: Load auto-discovered connection map from Operation 6A (or re-run 6A first).
Step 2: Confirm OPTO_CNTR = HIGH (DAC current-source path active).
Step 3: For each discovered connection (HS[i] → LS[j]):
◦ Enable MUX_HI (CH_HI) and MUX_LO (CH_LO) as per the connection address.
◦ Enable DAC8775 Channel A output (10 mA). Current flows: DAC → MUX → harness wire → return.
◦ Read the Matrix ADC (SPI1, HI_COM): V_drop across the harness wire.
◦ Calculate: R_wire = V_drop / 0.010 Ω.
◦ Disable DAC output (Channel A = 0), disable both MUXes.
◦ Log: HS[i] → LS[j] : V_drop = X mV, R = Y Ω.
Step 4: After all measurements: set OPTO_CNTR = LOW (restore 3.3 V pull-up mode).
Step 5: Output: Resistance map of all 256 harness connections.
7B. Operation B — Pre-Defined Plan Resistance Verification
Purpose: Verify resistance of each connection in the pre-defined harness plan is within specified limits.
Step 1: Load harness plan with resistance limits: (HS[i] → LS[j], R_min, R_max) for all connections.
Step 2: Confirm OPTO_CNTR = HIGH (DAC current-source path active).
Step 3: For each connection in the plan:
◦ Enable MUX_HI and MUX_LO. Enable 10 mA current source.
◦ Read ADC: V_drop. Calculate R_wire = V_drop / 0.010.
◦ Compare:
    PASS             : R_min ≤ R_wire ≤ R_max
    HIGH RESIST FAIL : R_wire > R_max  (partial break, corrosion) → F08
    LOW RESIST FAIL  : R_wire < R_min  (parallel path or cal error) → F09
◦ Disable current source and both MUXes.
Step 4: After all measurements: set OPTO_CNTR = LOW.
Step 5: Generate resistance test report: per-connection measured value, limits, PASS/FAIL status.
